Publication Ethics & Research Integrity

ResearchPro Academic Services is committed to responsible, transparent and ethical academic publishing practices. Our support is designed to help researchers communicate genuine academic work while respecting the standards and policies established by journals, publishers, institutions and scholarly organizations.

Our commitment to research integrity

We value originality, honesty, transparency, accurate reporting, proper citation, responsible authorship, respect for intellectual property, appropriate methodology, confidentiality and ethical scholarly communication.

Originality and plagiarism

Authors are responsible for ensuring that submitted manuscripts represent their own genuine research. Manuscripts should not contain plagiarized content, fabricated information, falsified results, manipulated data, unauthorized use of copyrighted material or improperly attributed ideas. All sources should be acknowledged and cited appropriately.

A similarity result should be interpreted in context. It does not by itself determine whether academic misconduct has occurred.

Authorship

All individuals listed as authors should have made an appropriate contribution under the target journal’s requirements. Authors are responsible for accurate author details, confirming author order, obtaining approval from all listed authors, declaring relevant conflicts and acknowledging contributors appropriately. ResearchPro does not determine authorship on behalf of researchers.

Data integrity

Authors are responsible for the accuracy and authenticity of their data, analysis, results, figures, tables and conclusions. ResearchPro does not support fabrication, falsification, manipulation or misrepresentation of research findings.

Peer review

Peer review is controlled by the relevant journal or publisher. ResearchPro may help authors understand reviewer comments and prepare revisions, but does not control editorial decisions or guarantee acceptance.

Conflicts of interest, confidentiality and corrections

Authors should disclose relevant relationships where required by the target journal. Manuscripts, unpublished research information and author details are handled with appropriate confidentiality and professional care. Errors or publication concerns should be addressed under the policies of the relevant journal or publisher; ResearchPro can provide administrative or publication-support assistance where applicable.

No guarantee of publication

ResearchPro provides research and publication-support services. Acceptance, peer review, publication and indexing decisions belong to the respective journal, publisher, editorial board or indexing organization. We do not guarantee these outcomes.

Refund & Payment Policy

This policy applies to services provided by ResearchPro Academic Services, an initiative of SriVelocity Business Solutions Private Limited. Before paying, customers should understand the service scope, charges, expected deliverables and conditions.

Payment and service commencement

Payments may be collected through the methods made available by ResearchPro, including online payment gateways, bank transfers or other approved methods. Applicable charges will be communicated before the service is confirmed. Taxes such as GST may apply in accordance with applicable law.

Work may begin after payment is confirmed and required documents or information are received. Refund eligibility after work begins may depend on the service, work completed, deliverables provided, third-party charges, administrative costs and the terms communicated before purchase.

Refund eligibility

Refund requests may be considered if a service has not started, ResearchPro cannot provide the agreed service, a duplicate payment was made, or an incorrect payment is identified and verified. Requests are assessed case by case under the applicable service terms.

Services that may become non-refundable

Customized work—including manuscript editing, academic formatting, research support, documentation, journal-selection research, manuscript preparation, publication support and completed consultancy—may become non-refundable once the agreed work has started or been delivered.

Journal rejection and third-party charges

Journal rejection does not automatically qualify for a refund. ResearchPro’s professional service fees are separate from a journal’s editorial decision. Third-party charges are governed by the relevant provider’s refund policy; ResearchPro cannot guarantee a refund of charges already paid or incurred.

Refund processing and cancellation

Approved refunds will normally be sent through the original payment method, subject to bank and payment-provider timelines. Customers wishing to cancel should contact ResearchPro as soon as possible. Requests are reviewed based on the stage and nature of the service.

Privacy Policy
