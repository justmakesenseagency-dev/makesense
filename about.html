About ResearchPro

Helping researchers communicate their work.

ResearchPro Academic Services is an academic research and publication-support initiative of SriVelocity Business Solutions Private Limited, India.

We support researchers, academicians, scholars, students and professionals with writing guidance, editorial assistance, manuscript preparation, journal-selection guidance, formatting and publication support.

Academic publishing can require careful attention to journal scope, formatting, citations, peer review, research integrity and submission requirements. Our aim is to make those practical steps clearer and more organized.

Our team supports work across Management, Business, Education, Social Sciences, Engineering, Technology, Medicine, Law, Humanities and related fields.

OUR VISION

An accessible research-support ecosystem

We aim to help researchers communicate their work effectively, reduce technical and language barriers, encourage responsible publishing and build long-term trust in the academic community.

OUR MISSION

Clear, structured, researcher-focused support

We provide professional guidance to improve manuscript clarity, help authors understand journal requirements, organize publication steps and support ethical scholarly communication.

What guides our work

Professional support, with clear boundaries.

Our role is to support researchers in preparing and communicating their own scholarly work.

01

Researcher-owned work

Researchers remain responsible for the ideas, data, interpretation, conclusions and academic submission.

02

Transparent guidance

We distinguish our support services from the decisions controlled by journals, publishers and indexing organizations.

03

Responsible attribution

We encourage originality, accurate citations, appropriate authorship and proper acknowledgement of contributors.

Important: We do not promise journal acceptance, peer-review outcomes, publication or database indexing. These decisions belong to the relevant independent organizations.
