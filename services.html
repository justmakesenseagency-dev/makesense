Academic support for every stage of your research.

Research Paper Publication Guidance

End-to-end preparation and process guidance for submitting to a potentially suitable journal.

  • Journal selection guidance
  • Manuscript formatting and submission preparation
  • Cover-letter assistance
  • Reviewer-response and revision support
SERVICE

Systematic Literature Review

Support to plan and present a structured literature review using a recognized review methodology.

  • Research question and search strategy
  • Screening framework and eligibility criteria
  • Literature synthesis and review structure
  • Reference management

Similarity Screening

Review textual similarity and receive guidance on citations, attribution and original expression. Similarity scores require contextual interpretation.

  • Review citation and quotation practices
  • Improve clarity without disguising copied work

Manuscript Proofreading

  • Formatting and reference checks
  • Final presentation review

Peer-Style Manuscript Review

  • Research clarity and structure

  • Results and discussion presentation
  • References and journal-readiness
