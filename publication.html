Publication support

Supporting researchers from research to publication.

ResearchPro Academic Services helps authors prepare genuine research for submission to suitable scholarly journals and publication platforms.

Researchers often need to manage journal selection, manuscript formatting, academic language, citations, submission steps and reviewer comments. We provide structured assistance across those stages.

  • Manuscript preparation, editing and journal-specific formatting.
  • Journal selection guidance based on topic, scope and article type.
  • Submission materials, reviewer responses and revision support.
  • Publication-process and research-visibility guidance.
OUR COMMITMENT

Clear support. Independent journal decisions.

We help authors understand requirements and prepare their work. We do not guarantee acceptance, peer-review outcomes, publication or indexing. Final decisions belong to the relevant journal, publisher, editorial board or indexing organization.

Call for research papers

Share original scholarly work.

Researchers, academicians, faculty members, students and professionals can contact us about publication-support options for original research.

We welcome enquiries across engineering, technology, management, business, finance, healthcare, life sciences, law, education, psychology, social sciences, humanities and environmental studies.

  • Work should represent genuine research and accurate reporting.
  • References and citations should be complete and appropriate.
  • Submissions should follow the relevant journal’s guidelines and ethics requirements.
  • Research data must not be fabricated, falsified or manipulated.
Submission is not acceptance. ResearchPro is a support service, not a journal. Any publication opportunity depends on the relevant journal’s scope, editorial policies, peer review and final decision.
